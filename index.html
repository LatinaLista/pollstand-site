The privacy promise

We can't leak what we never collect.

Most poll tools quietly store an email next to every answer and call the poll "anonymous" because they don't publish it. Ours is built so the pairing never exists — not in the page, not in the database, not in the spreadsheet.

  • No email required to vote. The vote is the only thing submitted.
  • Opt-in emails travel separately. A different request, a different list, never beside an answer.
  • The public results feed carries votes only. Timestamp and answer. Nothing else is in it to expose.
  • Need attribution instead? Member votes and board decisions are a different product. We'll build that too — and it says so on the page.
  • Labeled as an opinion poll. Every vote page and results board says so in plain sight. Your audience knows what they're looking at, and so does anyone who quotes it.
